## 📝 **App Description**

```
FiftyList is your personal companion for tracking books and movies. Set yearly goals, organize your reading and watching lists, and discover new content through intelligent suggestions.

FEATURES:
• Set and track yearly reading/watching goals
• Organize items by status: Completed, In Progress, Planned, or Abandoned
• Smart search with Google Books integration
• Intelligent suggestions based on your preferences
• Comprehensive statistics and progress tracking
• Export your complete list for backup or sharing
• Import existing lists with bulk import feature
• Beautiful, intuitive interface designed for productivity

PERFECT FOR:
• Book lovers tracking their reading goals
• Movie enthusiasts organizing their watchlist
• Students managing required reading
• Anyone wanting to build better reading/watching habits

Track your progress, discover new content, and achieve your goals with FiftyList - the ultimate reading and movie tracking app.

Download now and start building your perfect list!
```
