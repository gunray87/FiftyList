Privacy Policy for FiftyList

Effective Date: August 12, 2025

Introduction

FiftyList ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

No Personal Information Collected: FiftyList does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from your device.

Local Data Storage: All your reading and movie lists, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device using your device's secure storage.

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no information to use, share, or sell.

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All app data is stored locally on your device
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not use cloud services to store your data
  • No Data Transmission: Your data never leaves your device
  • Device Security: Your data is protected by your device's built-in security measures

Third-Party Services

FiftyList may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Books API: Used for book search functionality. We do not store or transmit any search queries to our servers.
  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and updates.

Children's Privacy

FiftyList does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, this is not applicable.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
