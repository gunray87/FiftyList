# Privacy Policy for FiftyList

**Effective Date**: August 12, 2025

## Introduction

FiftyList ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

**No Personal Information Collected**: FiftyList does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from your device.

**Local Data Storage**: All your reading and movie lists, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device using your device's secure storage.

## How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no information to use, share, or sell.

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: All app data is stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not use cloud services to store your data
- **No Data Transmission**: Your data never leaves your device
- **Export Feature**: You can export your data for backup purposes, but this is done locally on your device

## Third-Party Services

**Google Books API**: When you search for books, the app may connect to Google Books API to retrieve book information. This connection is made directly from your device and we do not store or process this information.

**TMDB API**: When you search for movies, the app may connect to TMDB API to retrieve movie information. This connection is made directly from your device and we do not store or process this information.

## Children's Privacy

FiftyList does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed to be safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: support@fiftylist.app

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. Your data is stored locally on your device and you have full control over it.
